# ALIGN
A participatory design method for Accountability-Control Alignment in High-Risk AI use cases 

Characteristics of Artificial Intelligence (AI) severely restrict human control. This creates new challenges for socio-technical systems design especially in safety-critical domains, where control over risks is essential along with the accountability for any harm caused.
Drawing from human factors and AI governance literature, expert interviews and participant observation in a complex high-risk AI use case from the railways, we developed ALIGN, a **scenario-based method that supports the governance of and stakeholder involvement in
AI development and implementation** in three ways: raising awareness for potential accountability-control misalignments in socio-technical
systems and their negative consequences, offering a structured approach to assessing the alignment between accountability and control for stakeholders in a given use case, and supporting effective design decisions to mitigate AI risks.

**Who Is ALIGN For?**

ALIGN is designed to support

Product owners and project managers of high-risk AI use cases
AI development teams and practitioners exploring questions around accountability and control within their organisations
AI regulators to reflect on AI risks and governance questions


No technical background is required to run or participate in an ALIGN workshop.

Have you used ALIGN? We'd love to hear about it. Share your experience by opening a Discussion.

**How to Use ALIGN**

Format: stakeholder workshop, 3–8 participants
Duration: approx.2-2.5 hours
Materials needed: slide deck, card set (digital or physical), printed stakeholder canvas (A3 or A2 recommended, one canvas per stakeholder), pens, post-its, flipchart or whiteboard 

**Structure**
Part 1 - theoretical introduction (approx. 15 minutes)
Use the slide deck to introduce participants to the concept of accountability-control alignment and the purpose of the workshop.

Part 2 - Scenario-based discussion of
control-accountability
misalignment in different safety-critical domains
(approx. 45-60 minutes)

Part 3 - Discussion of control- accountability
alignment in the particular use case of the workshop participants (approx. 45-60 minutes)

Facilitation: The slide deck includes relevant information and notes for each step.
